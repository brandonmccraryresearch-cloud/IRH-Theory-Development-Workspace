# IX.2 — Modeling Decisions: Research Brief

**Paper:** §IX.2. **Character:** decisions, not derivations. The paper is explicit:
the dimensional assignment "must be decided. It is not resolvable by further
calculation." This brief exists to keep the decision visible and to record the
arguments as they develop — not to compute an answer that computation cannot give.

## 2.1 The dimensional assignment

The framework carries three structures that need placing: a four-dimensional
base, an internal $S^3$, and an observed 3+1 spacetime. Three mutually exclusive
readings:

### (A) The base is Euclidean spacetime; Lorentzian signature arises dynamically.

- **Most economical.** The only reading consistent with citing four-dimensional
  optimality as evidence *about spacetime*.
- **Cost:** requires the signature mechanism of §IX.3 (problem 3.1) — and the
  paper notes no purely classical Euclidean substrate is known to yield
  Lorentzian signature. Reading (A) therefore stands or falls with 3.1.
- **What would strengthen it:** a concrete dynamical-signature construction
  adapted to the Swift–Hohenberg substrate (cf. Mukohyama–Uzan [17],
  Kehayias–Mukohyama–Uzan [18]).

### (B) The base is four spatial dimensions with one compactified.

- **Self-consistent:** the cross-section result (§II.10) leaves FCC in three
  dimensions.
- **Cost:** requires a compactification mechanism; predicts Kaluza–Klein modes;
  and — the sharpest cost — compactification **breaks the D₄ symmetry the
  selection argument uses**. The S-criterion selected D₄ in 4D; singling out one
  direction for compactification has to be justified without circularity.
- **What would strengthen it:** a dynamical compactification mechanism (cf.
  problem 1.5 — irrational projections may bear on this); a KK-mode phenomenology
  with a falsifiable mass scale.

### (C) The base is auxiliary; spacetime emerges from defect dynamics.

- **Most speculative; no construction offered.** The paper lists it for
  completeness.
- **Cost:** everything — there is no metric, no causal structure, no dynamics
  until the construction exists.
- **What would strengthen it:** any concrete construction at all. Until one
  exists, (C) is a placeholder, not a contender.

## 2.2 Generation count as input

**Standing rule.** Three generations is an INPUT (§VIII.3, §V.2 verdict), not an
output. Five candidate mechanisms were tested and closed; the ℤ₃ coincidences
(coset structure, centre grading) are real symmetries that do not generate
multiplicity.

Wherever fermion content is discussed in new work:
- State "three generations assumed" explicitly.
- Do not present the 8+8+8 coset decomposition or the centre ℤ₃ as an
  explanation of family replication. The paper calls this conflation "seductive"
  and it is now a known trap — see `guardrails/EXCLUDED.md`.

**Open sub-question (legitimate):** *given* three generations as input, how do
they embed in the E₆ → SO(10) → SU(5) → SM breaking chain? That is problem 4.1,
not a reopening of the derivation question.
