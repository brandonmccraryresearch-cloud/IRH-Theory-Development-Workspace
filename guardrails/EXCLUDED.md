# EXCLUDED — Do Not Revive

Claims v93.1 excludes by theorem or retracts with stated cause (Parts VII,
VIII.2). If new work appears to require any of these, the new work is wrong —
find the error. This list is closed; it grows only by new exclusion theorems,
never by convenience.

## Excluded by theorem

- **𝔰𝔬(8) as gauge algebra** — two independent proofs (§IV.1): chirality
  ($-1 \in W(D_4)$, all representations real) and embedding (Borel–de
  Siebenthal: no maximal-rank subalgebra contains 𝔰𝔲(3)⊕𝔰𝔲(2)).
- **Standard Model embedding in 𝔰𝔬(8)** by any route; **Pati–Salam from
  𝔰𝔬(8)** (rank 5 cannot come from rank 4; the v92 cascade also increased
  dimension 15→21).
- **Kerr solitons in 4D** — Derrick's theorem (§III.4).
- **Skyrmions, hopfions, gauge structure from a single real scalar** — the
  phase sector has no non-translational DOF left after triad locking; the
  homotopy is $\pi_1=\mathbb{Z}^4$, $\pi_2=\pi_3=0$ (§IV.2). Internal structure
  is forced, not optional.
- **Three generations as derived** — five mechanisms tested and closed (§V.2).
- **Monopole absence** — inconsistent with E₆ breaking topology (§V.3).
- **Universal optimality of D₄** — a lower-symmetry competitor wins in a narrow
  window (Cohn–Conway–Elkies–Kumar [13], confirmed independently) (§II.11).

## Retracted (v92 claims withdrawn for cause)

- $\xi_2 = -1/384$ anisotropy coefficient — not derivable from the stated
  lattice (the D₄ fourth moment is exactly isotropic).
- Both v92 time steps ($\Delta t = a_0/c$, $\Delta t = T_P$) — violate the
  derived CFL bound.
- Three generations from triality.
- Original Schrödinger emergence — carrier degenerate ($e^{-i\theta s}=1$);
  replaced by the corrected §III.3 envelope equation (which stands).
- Lorentzian signature from $i^2=-1$ — invalid; canonical quantization is
  complex in Euclidean field theory too.
- Einstein equations from elasticity — analogy, not derivation.
- Higgs VEV, A₃ mass/widths, CKM — computed in an excluded framework with
  independent arithmetic errors. Nothing carried forward.
- "Zero empirical calibration", "100% closure" — not defensible in any version.
