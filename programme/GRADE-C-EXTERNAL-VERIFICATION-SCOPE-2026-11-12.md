## 0. The grade boundary — read first

**External verification is still artificial (I1), so Track E stays capped at C
by the $I1\toC$ independence bound.** Outside verification corroborates or tests
grade-C claims; it does not lift them above C. A Co-scientist basin map, a
Manus retrieval sweep, or a Gemini 4 check that comes back confirming a
manuscript claim leaves that claim at grade C — the verification is
artificial-only, and no grade above C is available on that evidence. What
outside verification CAN do: confirm, correct, or refute a grade-C claim;
supply the second independent route that lifts a **Track F** item to F2 (per
the §6.5 repeal — e.g. the Compare $D_{4}$ counts, $F3 \to F2$, and WS-D task-12
G1/G5/G8/G12, $F3 \to F2$, both already regraded on that basis); and close a gap
with a graded verdict (verified / corrected / refuted / ABSTAIN), which is
what "closed" means in this programme — not vindication of the manuscript.

The three agents are the fully authorized method for outside verification.
Each interaction must be seen all the way through to its result and audited
locally claim-by-claim on arrival. Deliverables are reports, figures, and
tables only.
