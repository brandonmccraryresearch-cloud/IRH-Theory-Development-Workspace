## 3. Gaps and untested conditions (consolidated list)

### 3a. IRH's own gaps (each traceable to the manuscript's own text)

1. **Signature:** no mechanism under any reading (A)/(B)/(C). Untested: whether any purely classical Euclidean substrate can yield Lorentzian signature (manuscript: none known).
2. **Gravity:** no Einstein equations, diffeomorphism invariance, universal coupling, or two graviton polarisations; impedance-tensor metric is effective only.
3. **Constants:** triad definitional; $\alpha$ ansatz numerological; R = 2589 subtraction unforced; $\bar{\psi}$ unfixed; $q_{0}$ tuning unfixed. Untested: $D_{4}$ band under simultaneous detuning + higher-order terms (problem 1.3 / prediction VI.2).
4. **Dimensional assignment:** undecided; (B) predicts KK modes with no KK spectrum computed in IRH; (C) has no construction.
5. **Quantum mechanics:** envelope Schrödinger equation only (§III.3, validity $a_{0}$|k| ≲ 0.3 at <1% error); entanglement/Bell needs nonlocality or superdeterminism.
6. **Matter sector:** no $E_{6}$ breaking pattern, no Higgs sector, no CKM/VEV (all §IX.4 "must be restarted"); monopole resolution missing (VI.7 / problem 3.4); three generations is an input.
7. **Cosmology:** no nucleation mechanism; joint early-universe narrative missing in its entirety; 11.85% vs 11.90% band discrepancy unresolved (author decision pending).
8. **No IRH prediction touches $\alpha$, signature, or Einstein's equations** — the three questions this task compares. The comparison therefore cannot be settled by IRH's own predictions.

### 3b. Rivals' gaps (as they bear on IRH)

9. Greensite: one-loop, massless-field, fermion-dominance premises; higher-loop / massive-field robustness and current standing ABSTAIN.
10. Mukohyama–Uzan: clock field + fine-tuned coupling (secondary criticism recorded, not adopted); Galileon-not-GR gravity; quantum stability not assessed here.
11. Analog gravity: no route to Einstein equations in any surveyed model; multi-component Lorentz-invariance difficulty (Sindoni §4); analogue Hawking results are per-system, not gravity.
12. Varying-$\alpha$: current observational status ABSTAIN; early Webb et al. value $(\Delta\alpha/\alpha \approx -0.72\times10^{-5}$, as cited in period secondary literature) is not a current consensus statement.
13. Anthropic: ensemble unobserved; typicality assumed; Weinberg's galaxy-number-counts test not assessed here.
14. Eddington: failed — the control case for what a failed $\alpha$ derivation looks like.
15. $E_{6}$ unification: breaking chain and spectrum are inputs; absolute coupling values are inputs; proton-decay / unification-scale tensions are background, not re-verified here.
16. Causal set: Hauptvermutung unproved; no complete quantum dynamics; no matter coupling; no constants account; regular-lattice (IRH) vs random-sprinkling (CST) Lorentz-invariance strategies are incompatible as stated.
17. NCG: specific coupling/Higgs numbers ABSTAIN; unimodularity condition reported as ad hoc (secondary); Lorentzian formulation not assessed here.

### 3c. Shared / structural gaps (no party holds the conjunction)

18. No surveyed account derives $\alpha^{-1}$ from first principles and survives measurement.
19. No surveyed account derives Lorentzian signature *and* the Einstein equations *and* the constants from a single substrate.
20. No shared, discriminating prediction exists between IRH and any rival in this survey (HLRE I2+ comparison not possible on the present record) — so no ranking of IRH vs rivals is licensed by this draft. The matrix codes, it does not crown.
21. **Bartha summary:** every derivation-transfer attempted from a rival into IRH FAILS (Greensite, Mukohyama–Uzan, analog-GR, $E_{6}$-couplings, anthropic-tunings). The positive analogies that survive are *suggestive parallels* ("both read physics off geometry", "both start Euclidean") — grade D as claims about either framework, motivating nothing further without an independent test.
