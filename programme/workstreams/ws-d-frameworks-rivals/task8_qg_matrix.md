## 4. Gaps / untested conditions (consolidated list)

### 4.1 Shared gaps (all frameworks)

1. **No confirmed Planck-scale prediction** except causal sets' heuristic
   $\Lambda$ order-of-magnitude (C12, grade C).
2. **Continuum / classical limit unproved** wherever it is needed
   (LQG, CDT, GFT, causal sets).
3. **de Sitter quantum gravity**: no controlled construction in any
   framework; AdS/CFT is AdS-only; CDT's de Sitter is Euclidean-simulated.
4. **Lorentz violation bounds**: every emergent/discrete framework must
   explain the observed absence; IRH's quantitative target (6th-order
   anisotropy size) is uncomputed.

### 4.3 Rival-framework gaps (one line each — detail in the draft §5)

- **LQG:** classical limit; uniqueness of dynamics; matter/gauge by hand.
- **String/AdS-CFT:** proof of duality; de Sitter; experimental signature;
  landscape falsifiability.
- **Asymptotic safety:** fixed point beyond truncations; finite relevant
  directions; sharp predictions.
- **CDT:** continuum limit; matter coupling; Lorentzian observables.
- **Causal sets:** quantum dynamics; GR recovery; $\Lambda$ fluctuation test.
- **GFT:** condensate = GR geometry; geometrogenesis without extra time;
  non-mean-field condensate.
- **Analogue/emergent:** single metric + equivalence principle; Jacobson
  premises in a concrete substrate; dynamics (not just kinematics).

### 4.4 What each framework would need from the others (cross-references)

- IRH needs from **analogue gravity** the honest kinematics/dynamics
  split (already adopted, §IX.3) and from **Greensite et al.** a
  signature mechanism it cannot supply itself — currently FAILS (§6.3
  of the draft).
- CDT needs from **asymptotic safety** the RG language for its continuum
  limit (spectral-dimension running is already RG-like in spirit).
- GFT needs from **CDT** a demonstration that a condensate phase has a
  de Sitter-like continuum limit.
- LQG needs from **GFT** a second-quantized dynamics and from **CDT**
  numerical evidence about the continuum limit.
- Causal sets need from **everyone** a dynamics; they offer in return
  the only prediction-in-advance in the set.
- String theory needs from **CDT / causal sets** a de Sitter construction
  it does not have; it offers the only precise duality conjecture.
