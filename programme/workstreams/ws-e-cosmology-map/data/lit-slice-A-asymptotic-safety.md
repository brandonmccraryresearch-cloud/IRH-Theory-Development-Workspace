## Fixed-point evidence status (as the retrieved sources state it)

- The Reuter fixed point has accumulated evidence across many truncations (Einstein–Hilbert, f(R), higher-derivative) and methods (FRG, lattice CDT, perturbative $2+\varepsilon$ and 4D), with only a few relevant directions and near-canonical scaling of irrelevant operators (Held et al. 2025, arXiv:2504.12006; Schiffer 2025, arXiv:2509.26352). No proof exists; all evidence is truncation-dependent.
- The number of relevant directions is small (Eichhorn 2023 states three free parameters for AS gravity); the exact count is truncation- and matter-dependent, not a settled number.
- Lorentzian formulations are now threefold and converging: covariant Lorentzian FRG (D'Angelo 2024; D'Angelo–Ferrero–Fröb 2025 on de Sitter), foliated/ADM Wick-rotated FRG (Saueressig–Wang 2025), and spectral FRG graviton spectral functions (Fehre et al. 2023; Pawlowski et al. 2025). All are at Einstein–Hilbert-truncation level for the fixed-point claim.
- The Branchina et al. measure challenge (2024) and the Held et al. reply (2025) are both live in the literature; the map should record the fixed point as "strong truncation evidence, contested on measure grounds" rather than "established".

## Emergent / analogue spacetime and pattern selection in AS (direct answer to the task's last clause)

- AS proper has **no emergent-spacetime claim**: spacetime (the metric) is the fundamental quantum field, quantised directly. The nearest analogues are (a) spectral-dimension flow $4\to2$ in the UV (a property of the fixed-point regime, not an emergent substrate) and (b) CDT, where a de Sitter-like geometry emerges dynamically from discrete triangulations and the spectral dimension flows $4\to2$ — but CDT is a lattice regulator/method, and its UV fixed point is unproven (Ambjørn et al. 2024).
- **No pattern-selection mechanism** comparable to IRH's triad-resonance selection exists in AS. The fixed point selects *trajectories* (a finite-dimensional critical surface) and *excludes* some matter contents (e.g. Weinberg operator for neutrinos; some scalar-tensor fixed points ruled out by inflation), which is selection *of couplings/models*, not selection of a spatial pattern or lattice from alternatives.
- **No analogue-gravity work** within AS was found in this retrieval; AS does not model spacetime as an emergent condensed-matter-like medium.

## Overall verdict vs the three criteria (preliminary, for the map)

1. **Substrate ontology — FURTHEST from IRH.** AS quantises the metric directly; there is no physical substrate or microstructure from which geometry emerges. Only the CDT lattice flank has discrete building blocks, and those are a regulator, not a claimed physical substrate. IRH's substrate-first ontology has no counterpart here.
2. **Selection mechanism — PARTIAL / DIFFERENT KIND.** AS has a genuine dynamical selection principle (the UV fixed point + finite critical surface), and it does real selection work: it excludes matter contents and fixes couplings. But it selects among RG trajectories/couplings, not among spatial patterns, lattices, or vacua. It is the strongest "selection" of any QFT-based approach in the map, and the weakest match to IRH's *pattern* selection.
3. **Testable predictions — STRONGEST in the map so far (on paper).** AS states quantitative, falsifiable predictions: the Higgs mass $\approx 126$ GeV (Shaposhnikov–Wetterich 2010, pre-discovery), the seesaw-scale bound ~$10^{14}$ GeV and visible-neutrino-mass bound (de Brito et al. 2025), proton-decay suppression to zero at the fixed point (Eichhorn & Ray 2024), and inflationary ruling-out of two of three scalar-tensor fixed points (Koch et al. 2026). Caveats: the Higgs prediction is now framed as partly post-dictive and truncation-sensitive; none of the others has been tested. IRH by contrast states no GW/CMB prediction at all — so AS out-scores IRH on criterion (3) while being furthest from it on criterion (1).
