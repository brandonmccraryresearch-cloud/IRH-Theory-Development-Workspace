## Preliminary structural assessment (for the workstream-E synthesis; NOT a verdict)

- **Substrate ontology:** the genuine substrate holders are GFT condensate cosmology (B12, B13, B15), the Wolfram model (B22, B23), analogue gravity (B20, B21 — literal but avowedly analogue), and Verlinde (B16, B17 — unspecified microscopic theory). LQG/spin foams, CDT and causal sets quantise geometry directly and have no substrate in IRH's sense; string theory has none either.
- **Selection mechanism:** the strongest dynamical-selection analogues are CDT's phase diagram (B3, B6, B7), causal-set path-integral suppression of non-manifoldlike causets (B9), and GFT condensation into a single-mode regime (B12) / interaction-order dictionary (B13). None selects a *specific* preferred pattern the way IRH's triad-resonance interference selects D4 (S = 64/141); the swampland (B24) selects only by exclusion, and the Wolfram model's causal invariance (B22) selects a regime, not a structure.
- **Testable predictions:** only three families state sharp, falsifiable numbers — Verlinde's fixed acceleration scale (B17, corrected B18), causal sets' everpresent $\Lambda (B8)$ and Higgs/Landau-pole shift (B10), and analogue Hawking radiation (B21, laboratory, not cosmological). GFT cosmology (B12, B13) is the only approach that, like IRH, derives BOTH dark-sector components from one mechanism — but from a condensate, not a resonant substrate — and its numbers are parameter-dependent.
- **Closest overall (preliminary):** GFT condensate cosmology (B12/B13) is structurally closest to IRH: it alone combines a real pre-geometric substrate (condensate of spacetime quanta) with a dynamical emergence mechanism (condensation) and quantitative cosmological predictions (dark sector, phantom crossing). The Wolfram model (B22) is the runner-up on substrate ontology alone but has no selection of a specific pattern and no predictions. CDT is the runner-up on selection (phase diagram) but has no substrate and no predictions. Verlinde is the runner-up on predictions (sharp, falsifiable) but its substrate is unspecified and it has no selection mechanism. No approach in this slice matches IRH on all three criteria simultaneously, and none has a D4-like discrete pattern-selection ratio.
